Meow Wishes

Every cat has a wish. Seat them so all of them are happy.

Need help?

Write to us at hello.manifiora@gmail.com — we usually answer within a few days. Please mention your device model and the level number if your question is about a puzzle.

Questions

How do I play?

Drag each cat onto a cushion. Tap a cat to see its wishes: next to the window, away from the fish bowl, beside a friend… “Next to” means the four sides only, not diagonals. When every cat is happy, the level is solved. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

I’m stuck on a level.

Tap the light bulb: a hint seats one cat in its correct place. You earn more hints and Paws by finishing levels and opening the daily gift.

What are Paws?

Paws are coins you earn by playing. Spend them in the Shop on café themes, cushions and outfits for your cats. They cannot be bought with real money.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) → Language, and pick one of the eight languages or “Device language”.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the App deletes the save, so keep it installed to keep your café.
