Privacy Policy

Meow Wishes · Effective and last updated November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manifiora (“we”, “us”), the developer and data controller, handles information when you use the Meow Wishes: Cat Logic Puzzle game for iOS (the “App”) and this website. If you have any questions, contact us at hello.manifiora@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads and no real-money purchases. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game save (progress, Paws and settings) is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous game statistics — for example which levels are started and completed — to make the puzzles better.
  • If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a technical crash report (nothing personal) so we can fix it.
  • You can turn both off at any time in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifier, and we never sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the App stores the following on your device only: completed levels, your Paws (in-game coins) and hints, the themes, cushions and accessories you unlocked with Paws, your cats’ outfits, your daily gift streak, tutorial status and settings (music, sound effects, vibration, language and usage data sharing). This save is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the App.

If you leave the language setting on “Device language”, the App reads your device’s preferred language to show the game in that language. This happens on your device only.

2. Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

To understand how the game is played and to improve it (for example, to find levels that are too hard), the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. It records a small set of game events: the App being opened and the screens you visit; a level being started or completed, with the level number, the time taken and whether a hint was used; hints, daily gifts and items unlocked or used with Paws; finishing the introduction; and changing the language.

With these events Google Analytics also receives technical information: a random ID that Firebase creates for this installation of the App, device model, operating system and App version, language, and your country or region (derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics does not store). It does not receive your name, email, contacts or game save, and the App is built without the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google Analytics’ advertising features are turned off: this data is never used for ads or for tracking you across other companies’ apps.

You can stop usage statistics at any time in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”.

3. Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

If the App crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google, sends us a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: the error and where in the App’s code it happened, the device model, operating system and App version, device state at the time (such as free memory and orientation), the time of the crash, a random installation ID and the game events from the previous section that happened just before the crash.

A crash report does not contain your name, email, contacts, game save or any advertising identifier. Crash reports are used only to keep the App stable. The same Settings switch (“Share anonymous usage data”) turns them off.

4. App settings from our server — Firebase Remote Config

When the App starts, it may download a few settings from Firebase Remote Config, a service of Google — for example whether a newer version of the App is available. To do this Firebase uses the random installation ID described above. No personal information is sent.

5. Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts, sign-in, chat, social features, user-generated content, push notifications, advertising or in-app purchases. Paws are earned by playing and cannot be bought. The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and it does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health or financial information.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information and we do not share any data ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google, acting as our service provider for the Firebase services described above. Apple processes data about your download of the App under its own privacy policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Children

The App is a calm puzzle game with no mature content, intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. We never have a copy, so we cannot view, restore or delete it for you.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for at most 14 months and then deleted; only aggregated reports without the installation ID remain.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and then deleted.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, normally no longer than 12 months.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, US state privacy laws or Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We process usage statistics and crash reports based on our legitimate interest in a working, improving game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; Article 5(2)(f) KVKK), and you can object at any time with the Settings switch or by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

10. International transfers

Google may process usage statistics and crash reports on servers outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Security

Your game data never leaves your device. Data collected by Firebase is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

12. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before the App’s data practices change — for example if we add a new service. The date at the top always shows the current version.
